# Editorial policy — Last Ride Registry

This policy governs every named case published on the site (the "Recent"
curated layer and, later, the verified-outcomes registry). FARS-derived rows
are anonymous and are not covered by it.

## What we publish

- **Facts only, cited.** Every claim on a case page traces to a published news
  report or an official court/police record, linked inline. No synthesis
  beyond neutral summary in our own words.
- **Victims are named** when named in cited coverage — this is a memorial.
  Minors: first name and last initial unless the family has publicly chosen
  otherwise (e.g., through a named foundation or public statements).
- **Drivers are named only when (a) formally charged and (b) named in the
  cited coverage or court record.** Before charges: "the driver" only.
- **Legal status is dated, never final.** Always "as of DATE, per SOURCE."
  The absence of reported charges is written as "no charges have been
  reported as of DATE" — never as "the driver faced no consequences."
- **Unconfirmed details are labeled.** Cases carry a `details_confirmed`
  flag; unconfirmed cases show a visible "details still being confirmed"
  notice.
- **No fault speculation, no graphic detail, no adjectives doing the
  grieving.** The facts are enough.

## Corrections and removal

- Factual errors: corrected with priority; the fix is visible in the
  repository's git history.
- Verified immediate family may request removal of a name; the case remains
  as an anonymized entry so counts stay honest.
- Contact: open an issue on the repository, or the correction email on the
  site.

## Sourcing bar

At least one reputable outlet (local newspaper, TV station, wire service) per
fact; two independent sources preferred per case. Social media posts,
fundraiser pages, and forums are never sole sources for contested facts.
